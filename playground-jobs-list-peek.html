How to try

  1. Click any job card to select it (click again to deselect).
  2. Scroll the list up/down.
  3. When the selected card leaves the viewport, the peek shows up at the top (if card is above) or bottom (if card is below).
  4. Click the peek to scroll back to the original card position (if enabled).
  5. Toggle "direction-aware" off to compare with a peek locked at the top.

Notes

  • The peek is a faithful clone of the JobCard (same accent, priority symbol, deadlines, buffer).
  • Clicking the peek does not toggle selection — that would deselect the job and defeat the purpose. It scrolls back to origin with a brief flash.
  • Direction-aware is the more informative default: the peek position tells you where to scroll to find the job.
